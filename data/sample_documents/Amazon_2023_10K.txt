FORM 10-K ANNUAL REPORT

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Net sales increased 12% to $574,785 million in 2023, compared with $513,983 million in 2022.
Operating income was $36,852 million, compared with operating income of $12,248 million in 2022.
Net income was $30,425 million, compared with a net loss of $2,722 million in 2022.
