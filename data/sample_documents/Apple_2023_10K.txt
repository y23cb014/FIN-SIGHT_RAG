APPLE INC.
For the fiscal year ended September 30, 2023

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations
During fiscal year 2023, Apple continued to navigate a challenging macroeconomic environment.
Total net sales were $383,285 million, compared to $394,328 million in fiscal 2022.
Gross margin was $169,148 million.
Operating income was $114,301 million.
Net income reached $96,995 million.
The Company returned over $77 billion to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases.
