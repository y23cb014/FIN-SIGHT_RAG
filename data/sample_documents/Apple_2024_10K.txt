APPLE INC.
For the fiscal year ended September 28, 2024

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations
Fiscal 2024 net sales grew by 2% to $391,035 million compared to $383,285 million in fiscal 2023.
Services revenue reached an all-time record of $96,169 million, driven by growth in advertising, cloud services, and payment services.
Gross margin increased to $180,683 million, representing a gross margin percentage of 46.2%.
Operating income expanded to $123,216 million.
Net income for fiscal 2024 was $93,736 million, reflecting an effective tax rate impacted by the European State Aid judgment.
