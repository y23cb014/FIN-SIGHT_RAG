APPLE INC.
PRELIMINARY UNAUDITED PRESS RELEASE & EARNINGS ADVISORY
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE - Q4 FY2024 INITIAL REPORT

Apple Reports Preliminary FY2024 Unaudited Financial Indicators:
Apple Inc. today announced preliminary, unaudited financial indicators for the fiscal year 2024.
Total preliminary net revenue was estimated at $388,500 million before final tax adjustments and foreign currency revaluations.
Services revenue was estimated at $94,500 million.
Note: These numbers reflect initial preliminary estimates and are subject to final audit reconciliations in the upcoming Form 10-K.
