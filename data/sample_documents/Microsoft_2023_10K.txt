Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Microsoft reported total revenue of $211,915 million for fiscal year 2023, compared to $198,270 million in 2022.
Gross profit was $146,052 million.
Operating income was $88,523 million.
Net income reached $72,361 million.
