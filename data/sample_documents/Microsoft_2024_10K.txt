MICROSOFT CORPORATION
For the fiscal year ended June 30, 2024

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
For fiscal year 2024, Microsoft achieved revenue of $245,122 million, an increase of 16% YoY.
Operating income increased 24% to $109,433 million.
Net income expanded to $88,136 million, up 22%.
Intelligent Cloud revenue grew 20% to $105,362 million, driven by Azure and other cloud services growth of 30%.
