NVIDIA CORPORATION
FORM 10-K ANNUAL REPORT
For the fiscal year ended January 28, 2024

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Fiscal year 2024 was a transformative year marked by the emergence of Generative AI.
Revenue grew 126% to $60,922 million.
Gross profit was $44,301 million (gross margin percentage of 72.7%).
Operating income rose to $32,972 million.
Net income increased to $29,760 million.
Data Center revenue surged 217% to a record $47,525 million, driven by the NVIDIA HGX platform and Hopper architecture.
