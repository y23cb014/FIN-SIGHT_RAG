# Private Analyst Research Memo: Big Tech AI Infrastructure Thesis
Author: Senior Technology Analyst
Date: October 2024
Subject: Comparative CapEx & Infrastructure Positioning: Apple, Microsoft, Amazon, NVIDIA

## Key Observations:
1. NVIDIA has captured unprecedented monopoly rents in accelerated computing, growing Data Center sales from $15.0B in FY23 to $115.2B in FY25.
2. Microsoft's Azure cloud integration has enabled it to monetize GenAI earlier than hyperscaler peers, maintaining operating margins above 44%.
3. Apple maintains the strongest consumer hardware ecosystem with iPhone net sales exceeding $201B in 2024, but services growth (+12.9% in 2024 to $96.2B) remains its premier margin driver.
4. Amazon's AWS reached an inflection point with $105.4B run-rate in 2024 while North America retail profitability expanded substantially.
